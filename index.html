Research Foundations

Through my research, I investigated how trust, access control, and cryptographic guarantees can be enforced across distributed architectures—such as Edge, Fog, and Cloud—under adversarial and resource-constrained conditions.

A significant part of my work focused on resource-constrained devices, where conventional security assumptions often break down. To support both practitioners and researchers, I developed a structured cryptographic taxonomy and reference document that analyzes the software and hardware performance of approximately 130 lightweight cryptographic primitives, including symmetric ciphers, asymmetric schemes, and hash functions. This work combines empirical performance evaluation with cryptanalytic insights, enabling informed security and implementation choices for real-world embedded and IoT systems.

Systems & Engineering Practice

I have designed, implemented, and evaluated end-to-end security mechanisms, from protocol logic and smart contracts to implementation and empirical measurement. This experience has provided me with knowledge in attacker thinking, failure modeling, and system-level reasoning.

From Research to Industry Security

Alongside my academic work, I am transitioning toward industry-based cybersecurity roles, driven by curiosity about how security operates in practice, within SOCs, vulnerability management pipelines, audits, compliance frameworks, and detection workflows.

I am actively building hands-on, industry-relevant experience in areas such as vulnerability management, log analysis, web application security, governance, risk, and compliance, and ISO 27001–aligned security controls. This is reinforced through practical labs and simulations on platforms such as TryHackMe, exposing me to blue-team, red-team, and GRC-oriented workflows.

Career Direction & Growth

My long-term aspiration is to grow into a security engineer, detection engineer, or security architect role—contributing both to day-to-day operational security and to long-term architectural decisions.

To support this path, I am working toward advanced certifications such as CISSP, CompTIA CySA+, and offensive security tracks (OSCP-style), complementing my academic background with industry-recognized validation.

For hiring teams, this means I bring analytical rigor and strong fundamentals, while also offering long-term value through deep system understanding and forward-looking security insight.
