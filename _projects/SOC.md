## Overview

This capstone project focused on the **operational requirements of security teams** across different organizations. The core task was to evaluate a set of applicants and **assemble a SOC team** by assigning appropriate **roles and responsibilities**. The project also included **hands-on reconnaissance**, where I performed network scanning activities to support monitoring and security operations workflows.

**Completion date:** 02 Jan 2026  
**Duration:** 1 hr 40 mins

---

## Objectives

By completing this project, I demonstrated the ability to:

- Distinguish between **standard security operations models**
- Assign **SOC roles and tasks** based on organizational needs and candidate profiles
- Perform **network reconnaissance** through scanning and basic discovery activities

---

## What I Did

### SOC team design and role assignment

- Reviewed SOC requirements for multiple organizational contexts
- Matched applicant capabilities to common SOC functions (triage, monitoring, escalation, and response support)
- Mapped tasks to roles to ensure coverage across monitoring and operational needs

### Security monitoring and reconnaissance

- Performed reconnaissance by scanning networks to identify exposed services and visibility gaps
- Connected scanning output to monitoring objectives (asset discovery and detection readiness)
