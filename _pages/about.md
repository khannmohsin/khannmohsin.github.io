<div style="margin-top: 10px;" class="justify-text">
I am a cybersecurity researcher working on secure, distributed, and dynamic systems, along with cryptography for the Internet of Things (IoT) and cyber-physical infrastructures.
</div>

My research focuses on:
- Blockchain-enabled trust enforcement for distributed IoT networks  
- Lightweight cryptography for resource-constrained devices  
- Side-channel resilience and secure protocol design  

<div style="margin-top: 10px;" class="justify-text">
I developed <strong>DTEA (Distributed Trust Enforcement Architecture)</strong> — a blockchain and smart contract–based framework using Proof-of-Authority consensus to enable policy-aware, secure communication across distributed (edge, fog, and cloud) nodes, aligned with NIST and OpenFog standards.
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 10px;" class="justify-text">
I have led benchmarking of 20+ lightweight hash functions and ciphers on AVR/ARM microcontrollers using ChipWhisperer, introducing E-RANK, a composite metric for performance-security trade-offs.  
I also conducted a survey of 116 lightweight cryptographic cipher, covering architecture, cryptanalysis, and implementation as well as security trade-offs.
</div>

My background includes:
- <strong>PhD</strong> (Thesis Phase) in Cybersecurity, UiT The Arctic University of Norway  
- <strong>M.Tech</strong> in Computer Science & Technology Engineering
- <strong>MBA</strong> in Operations Management  
- <strong>B.Tech</strong> in Information Technology & Telecommunication  Engineering 
- <strong>Industry experience</strong> as a Data Engineer at Blackbuck Insight, specializing in AWS, MapReduce, and cloud-native workflows

<div style="margin-top: 20px;" class="justify-text">
Currently, I am expanding my research toward post-quantum secure systems, with an emphasis on hybrid cryptographic models, standardized benchmarking, and ML-assisted threat detection.  
Alongside academic research, I am actively exploring industry opportunities in computer security sectors.
</div>
