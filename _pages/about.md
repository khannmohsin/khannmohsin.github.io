## ✦ Research Interests  
I work at the boundary where **distributed systems** meet **cyber-physical reality**, focusing on:

- Trust enforcement for IoT/OT systems across Edge → Fog → Cloud  
- Smart contract–driven access control and capability-token models  
- Lightweight cryptography and side-channel–aware hardware implementations  
- Threat modeling, vulnerability analysis, and resilient distributed protocols  

---

## ✦ BlockCap — Rethinking Trust for Distributed IoT  
<div style="margin-top: 10px;" class="justify-text">
At the heart of my research is <strong>BlockCap (Blockchain-Enabled Capability Access Protocol)</strong> — a security framework that reimagines how distributed IoT/OT systems establish trust.  
</div>

BlockCap brings together:

- Proof-of-Authority consensus  
- Event-driven validator logic  
- Dynamic capability-token policies  
- Secure node bootstrapping  
- Real-time observability  

<div style="margin-top: 5px;" class="justify-text">
Inspired by NIST 800-183 and OpenFog, BlockCap is designed for systems that are <em>too critical to trust blindly and too constrained to secure traditionally</em>.
</div>

## ✦ Security From the Hardware Up  
<div style="margin-top: 10px;" class="justify-text">
I benchmark lightweight cryptographic primitives on AVR and ARM microcontrollers using ChipWhisperer, measuring cycles, memory use, energy consumption, and leakage behavior. To evaluate trade-offs across diverse algorithms, I developed <strong>E-RANK</strong>, a holistic metric that captures efficiency, energy, and structural robustness in a single comparative score.
</div>

<div style="margin-top: 5px;" class="justify-text">
I also authored a structured survey of <strong>116 lightweight hardware hash functions</strong>, mapping their architectures, cryptanalytic insights, and implementation considerations — creating one of the most complete references in the field.
</div>
