My Story

My academic, professional, and research journey from Kashmir to Europe.

Early Years – Kashmir

I grew up in Kashmir, a region known for its natural beauty.
I completed my school and high school education in the science stream, focusing on mathematics and biology. From the beginning, I was fascinated by computers and technology — assembling PCs for friends, experimenting with hardware, and trying out programming languages like C and C#, often with help from my brother.

2014–2018 – Bachelor’s Degree

I pursued a Bachelor’s in Information Technology and Telecommunication Engineering, completing my thesis under Mr. Malik Mubashir Hassan.
He encouraged me and my peers to engage in research, explore new ideas, and consider opportunities beyond our local environment. His guidance helped me publish my first research paper during my undergraduate years and shaped my approach to learning and collaboration.

2018–2020 – Master’s in Computer Science

After a short role in web development, I began my Master’s in Computer Science, supervised by Dr. Bhavna Arora.
During this time, I published two research papers and attended a conference. The COVID-19 pandemic disrupted the final semester, but the extended timeline gave me the chance to complete my second paper and defend my thesis in October 2020.

In parallel, I enrolled in a Master of Business Administration (MBA) at IGNOU (distance learning) to strengthen my understanding of IT project management.

2020–2021 – Industry Experience

After my Master’s, I joined BBI Insights as a Data Engineer, working with TransUnion USA.
My role involved building data pipelines using AWS Cloud tools and Databricks (Python) to migrate data from on-premise servers to the cloud.
This position gave me valuable exposure to international collaboration and large-scale data infrastructure projects.

2021–Present – PhD at UiT The Arctic University of Norway

Driven by my ongoing interest in research, I applied for PhD positions in Europe.
I chose Europe to broaden my perspective, experience new cultures, and work with leading researchers.
I was accepted at UiT The Arctic University of Norway as a Cybersecurity Fellow, under the supervision of Prof. Håvard Johansen, Dag Johansen, and Elisavet Kozyri.
They have provided invaluable mentorship, guidance, and collaboration opportunities — leading to multiple publications and partnerships with internal UiT teams and external institutions like the University of Malaga.

Travel and Exploration

Beyond academics, I enjoy traveling, exploring local cultures, and trying different cuisines.
So far, I have visited:

I hope to complete my travels across Europe before exploring the rest of the world.
